rivacy Policy Last updated: 9 October 2026 Jeremy Home Bridge is a private smart-home integration operated solely for the app owner’s household. Information accessed The integration may access information made available through Google Nest Device Access for devices selected by the app owner. Depending on the device, this may include device names and metadata, live camera or doorbell streams, and supported events such as motion, person detection and doorbell presses. How the information is used This information is used only to provide household smart-home functionality through Homebridge and Apple Home, including camera viewing, doorbell notifications and device-event notifications. Storage and processing OAuth credentials and integration configuration are stored on the app owner’s private Homebridge host. Google Nest and Google Cloud process device streams and events as required to provide the Nest Device Access service. If Apple Home recording is enabled, Apple may process or store recordings according to the app owner’s Apple Home and iCloud settings. Sharing and sale of information Google Nest data is not sold, used for advertising or provided to data brokers. It is processed only by the services and software required to provide the household smart-home functionality described above. Access revocation and deletion Access can be revoked by removing the Google Partner Connection, revoking the Google account authorization, or deleting the relevant credentials and configuration from the Homebridge host.
