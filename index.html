Jeremy Home Bridge Jeremy Home Bridge is a private household smart-home integration. It connects selected Google Nest cameras and doorbells to Homebridge so they can be viewed and controlled through Apple Home. Purpose The integration uses Google Nest Device Access and Google Cloud Pub/Sub to receive supported camera streams and device events for devices belonging to the app owner’s household. This integration is for personal household use only. It is not offered as a public or commercial service. Google data use Google Nest data is accessed only to provide household smart-home functionality, including camera viewing, doorbell notifications and supported device-event notifications.
